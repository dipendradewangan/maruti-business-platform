# User Roles and Responsibilities

## Document Purpose

This document defines the initial user roles and responsibilities required to manage the customer solar project lifecycle.

The exact organizational structure and employee responsibilities will be confirmed with the client.

---

## Initial Role Structure

The initial system roles are based on the business activities identified during the first requirement discussion.

```text
Admin
  │
  ├── Customer Management
  ├── Document Verification
  ├── Government Registration
  ├── Banking Process
  ├── Installment Management
  ├── Product Management
  ├── Installation Management
  ├── Verification
  ├── Meter Installation
  ├── Subsidy Management
  └── Reports
```
